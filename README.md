# 🚗 FUN Hill Race Game 🏔️

A **2D Hill Climb Racing-style game** built in **Python** using **Pygame**. Drive your car across procedurally generated hills, collect coins, refuel at fuel stations, and avoid obstacles while balancing physics-based controls!

---

## ✨ **Features**
- 🏞️ **Procedural terrain generation** – endless unique hills
- ⛽ **Fuel system** – keep an eye on your fuel gauge!
- 💰 **Collectible coins** – increase your score
- 🚧 **Obstacles** – dodge or crash!
- 🏎️ **Physics-based car movement** – realistic slope interaction
- 🔧 **Car upgrades** – boost power, suspension & grip (via keys)
- 📏 **Distance & time tracking**
- 🎨 **Simple but clean 2D graphics**

---

## 🎮 **How to Play**
👉 **Controls:**
- `←` Move Left
- `→` Move Right
- `↑` Jump
- `P` Increase Power
- `S` Increase Suspension
- `G` Increase Grip
- `F` Refuel
- `R` Restart (after Game Over)

Stay fueled, climb as far as you can, and avoid crashing!
